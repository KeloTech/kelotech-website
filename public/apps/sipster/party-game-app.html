Best Party Game App for Adults: Sipster

Looking for a party game app, drinking game app, or truth or dare app that actually works for a real group? Sipster packs five adult party games into one iPhone app — no cards, no boards, no setup. Just add names and pass the phone.

What makes Sipster the best party game app for adults?

Most party game apps fail the moment you try them at a real hangout: everyone needs to download something, someone has to explain rules for ten minutes, or the questions run out by round three. Sipster was built for the opposite scenario — the pregame that starts in 30 seconds, the house party where nobody brought cards, the girls night that needs energy fast.

It is a single party game app for adults 17+ that combines:

  • Truth or dare (Party Mode) — every card calls a player by name
  • 18+ spin the bottle — digital wheel, spicy questions and dares
  • Never have I ever — built into Guys Night and Party Mode
  • Card-draw drinking games — Sipster mode replaces a full deck
  • Girls Night & Guys Night — gender-specific spin the bottle modes

All five modes share one player list (up to 20 names) on a single phone. Nobody else installs anything.

Drinking game app — with or without alcohol

Sipster works as a drinking game app and as a straight party game. Every challenge can hand out a penalty — a sip of whatever you're drinking, or a forfeit if you're playing dry. Designated drivers, non-drinkers and mixed groups stay in every round because alcohol is never required.

Truth or dare app & spin the bottle app in one

Instead of juggling a truth or dare app, a separate spin the bottle app, and a list of never-have-I-ever questions, Sipster runs all three from one screen. Switch modes mid-party without re-entering names — the same crew carries over from pregame to after-party.

Party Mode handles classic truth-or-dare. 18+ Spin the Bottle adds a digital wheel so the bottle never lands "between" two people or gets rigged. Girls Night and Guys Night tailor the deck to your group.

Games to play with friends on one phone

The best games to play with friends on one phone follow a pass-the-phone loop: draw, spin or answer, take the penalty, pass it on. Sipster automates whose turn it is, who gets targeted, and what the stakes are — which is why it scales from 4 friends to 20 without slowing down.
