FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people ask before downloading Sipster.

What is the best party game app for adults?

Sipster is a modern party game app for adults 17+ that combines truth or dare, 18+ spin the bottle, never have I ever, Girls Night and Guys Night games in one app. You add your friends' names, pick a game mode, and pass one phone around – no cards, boards or setup needed. Learn more →

What drinking games can you play with just your phone?

With Sipster you can play card-draw drinking games, truth or dare, spin the bottle and never have I ever using a single phone. Every challenge names a player and hands out penalties, so no cups, cards or ping pong balls are required. Learn more →

How do you play spin the bottle as an adult drinking game?

In Sipster's 18+ Spin the Bottle, every player's name goes on a digital wheel. Tap spin, and whoever the bottle lands on gets a spicy question or dare – complete it or take a penalty. It works for 2 to 20 players with one phone, and the digital bottle can never land on the same person "by accident" twice. Learn more →

Do you need alcohol to play Sipster?

No. Sipster works as a party game or a drinking game. Penalties can be sips of anything – water, soda or a drink – or swapped for silly forfeits, so designated drivers and non-drinkers can join every round. Play responsibly and respect everyone's limits. Learn more →

How many people can play Sipster at once?

Sipster supports up to 20 players on one phone. Add everyone's first names at the start and the games automatically call out players by name – which is exactly why it works so well for big parties where card games fall apart. Learn more →

Is Sipster free to download?

Yes, Sipster is free to download on the App Store. Full access to all game modes uses a Sipster Plus subscription, and the annual plan includes a free trial so you can test every mode before paying.

What games are good for a girls night at home?

Sipster's Girls Night mode is a girls-only spin the bottle with challenges and penalties built for a night in with your best friends – perfect for bachelorette parties, birthdays and galentines. Pair it with never have I ever for the full night. Learn more →

What is a good pregame drinking game for a party?

Fast, no-setup games work best for pregames. Sipster's Party Mode and card-draw mode start in seconds: add names, pass the phone, and every card gives someone a challenge or penalty before you head out. Learn more →

What are good truth or dare questions for adults?

Great adult truth or dare mixes light questions ("What's your most embarrassing text?") with spicier dares. Sipster's Party Mode and 18+ modes generate hundreds of them automatically, so nobody has to invent dares on the spot. Learn more →

Is Sipster safe – does it collect my data?

According to the App Store privacy label, the developer does not collect any data from this app. Player names you add stay on the phone. See the privacy policy for details.
