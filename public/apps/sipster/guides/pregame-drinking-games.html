Pregame Drinking Games to Start the Night Right

The Uber's in 45 minutes and the room needs energy. A good pregame drinking game starts instantly, includes everyone, and ends on time. Here's the playbook.

By the Sipster team · Updated July 2026

What makes a great pregame game

Pregames have a hard deadline, so the game has to hit fast: zero setup (nobody's setting up a pong table for 40 minutes of play), everyone included from round one, and pauseable – when the ride arrives, you just stop. Confession and challenge games beat skill games here; you want laughing and stories, not concentration.

The pregame lineup

1. Party Mode challenges (Sipster)

Add everyone's names, pass the phone, and the app calls people out: "Ethan, name every country you've visited and give out 1 penalty for each one." Challenges, truths, dares and votes – it's the highest laughs-per-minute format we know, and it starts in under a minute.

2. Never have I ever: speed round

Whole room plays every prompt at once, so it fits any group size and any time budget. Use our never have I ever questions or let the app deal them.

3. Most likely to

Point at the person "most likely to lose their phone tonight." Majority drinks. It doubles as a scouting report for the night ahead.

4. Card-draw rules (Sipster mode)

Draw a card, follow the rule. Some cards give out penalties, some create mini-rules that last the whole pregame ("no first names until you leave"). By the time the ride shows up, the group already has three inside jokes.

5. 18+ Spin the Bottle – if there's time

When the pregame is the party, escalate to the spicy wheel. Full rules in our spin the bottle guide.

The 45-minute pregame plan

  1. Minute 0–1: Download Sipster (free), add everyone's names.
  2. Minute 1–20: Sipster card mode or Party Mode – fast, mixed challenges while people finish getting ready.
  3. Minute 20–40: Group votes and never have I ever as everyone gathers.
  4. Minute 40+: Ride's here. Lock the phone, game over, no cleanup.

Pace matters at a pregame more than anywhere: penalties are sips, not shots, water rounds count, and non-drinkers play with whatever's in their cup. The goal is arriving at the party at your best – the app never requires alcohol.
