Party Games You Can Play With Just One Phone

The party's here, the vibe is right, and nobody brought cards. Good news: one phone in the room is all the equipment a great game night needs.

By the Sipster team · Updated July 2026

The "one phone" test

A true one-phone party game needs no cards, no board, no printouts, no second download and no "everyone scan this QR code." One device, passed around a circle, with the game doing the work. That rules out most party game apps – the good ones that pass the test are built around a pass-the-phone loop.

Five games, zero equipment

1. Pass-the-phone truth or dare

The app deals a truth or dare to a named player; they complete it or take a penalty; the phone moves on. Sipster's Party Mode is exactly this – see our adult truth or dare guide for what the cards look like.

2. Digital spin the bottle

No bottle, no flat surface, no arguments. Every player's name goes on a wheel; tap spin; the chosen player gets a challenge. Sipster ships three flavours: 18+ Spin the Bottle, Girls Night and Guys Night.

3. Card-draw drinking games

Somebody draws a card, the card names a player and a rule, penalties get handed out. The Sipster card mode replaces an entire deck of drinking game cards with one screen.

4. Never have I ever

Technically playable with no phone at all – until everyone runs out of ideas in round four. With the app dealing prompts, the game runs as long as the party. Steal starters from our question list.

5. Most likely to / group votes

The phone shows a prompt, everyone points at a player, majority takes the penalty. No equipment, infinitely scalable, dangerously fun with the right group.

Why one phone beats a bag of games

  • Setup is 30 seconds: add up to 20 names once; every mode uses them.
  • Nothing to buy, print or forget. The game is in your pocket at the airport, the cabin, the pregame and the after-party.
  • The app is the referee. Whose turn, who's targeted, what's the penalty – all decided for you, so nothing stalls.
  • Everyone plays, including non-drinkers. Penalties work with any drink or as forfeits – alcohol is never required.
