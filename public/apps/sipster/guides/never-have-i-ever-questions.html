Never Have I Ever Questions: Funny, Juicy & 18+

The perfect icebreaker needs zero props and thirty seconds of explanation. Here are 60+ never have I ever questions for every group – plus how to turn it into a drinking game that runs itself.

By the Sipster team · Updated July 2026

How to play never have I ever

Everyone holds up five (or ten) fingers. Players take turns saying "Never have I ever..." followed by something they've genuinely never done. Anyone who has done it puts a finger down. First player out of fingers loses.

The drinking game version is even simpler: skip the fingers – if you've done it, take a sip. If the whole group has done it, everyone drinks. Playing alcohol-free? Sips of anything count, or hand out points instead.

Funny never have I ever questions

Warm-up round

  • Never have I ever pretended to know a song and mouthed complete nonsense.
  • Never have I ever texted someone who was in the same room to avoid talking.
  • Never have I ever fallen asleep in a movie theater.
  • Never have I ever stalked an ex's new partner on social media.
  • Never have I ever eaten food that fell on the floor... past the five-second rule.
  • Never have I ever laughed so hard a drink came out of my nose.
  • Never have I ever forgotten someone's name mid-introduction.
  • Never have I ever waved back at someone who wasn't waving at me.

Juicy never have I ever questions

Getting personal

  • Never have I ever ghosted someone after more than three dates.
  • Never have I ever had a crush on a friend's sibling.
  • Never have I ever lied about my "number."
  • Never have I ever sent a risky text and immediately regretted it.
  • Never have I ever kissed someone whose name I didn't know.
  • Never have I ever gotten back with an ex I told everyone I was done with.

18+ never have I ever questions

With a close group of adults, the spicy tier is where fingers drop fast. Think club stories, hookup confessions and the things people only admit at 1 a.m. One example straight from Sipster's Guys Night mode: "Never have I ever... been so drunk I vomited in a car." If yes – take a penalty.

Play never have I ever with one phone

Reading questions off a listicle works until someone has to scroll, the phone locks, and the vibe dies. In Sipster, never have I ever prompts are dealt like cards:

  1. Add your players. Up to 20 names on one phone.
  2. Pick a mode. Never have I ever cards appear across modes – Guys Night and Girls Night tailor them to your crew, Party Mode keeps them mixed.
  3. Pass the phone. Cards flag whether the prompt is for one named player or for everyone, and the penalty is printed right on the card.

The app mixes never have I ever with dares, challenges and votes, so the game shifts before anyone gets bored of one format.
